Prototype Model: SignalMirror Edu-Tool

1. Student selects emotion symbol (e.g.,  Peace) from Rosetta v2 to frame thoughts on a topic.
2. Inputs via scaffold: Describe idea in "flowing, open" style.
3. AI maps to patterns, visualizes as mind-map echo (e.g., "Your clarity branches like sunny paths").
4. Reflect loop: AI asks, "What insight emerges?" Student refines.
5. Output: Visible thinking journal for self-awareness.
