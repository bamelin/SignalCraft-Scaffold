## 🧩 Conceptual Architecture

**Control → Regulation → Resonance**  
*(Box → Cooker → Mirror)*

### Control
- Reduces variance through consequence schedules.  
- Useful under crisis or uncertainty.  
- Failure mode: stagnation, coercion, overfitting.

### Regulation
- Adjusts dynamically to context via feedback loops.  
- Failure mode: over-adaptation, loss of structure.

### Resonance
- Anticipates before correction is needed (*empathy-as-feedback*).  
- Outcome: coherence and shared understanding.

---

## 🧠 Practice Protocol

### Control (Threshold Phase)
- Start when harm is plausible and time is thin.  
- Define minimum viable behaviors; make contingencies transparent.  
- Time-limit the Box — Boxes that overstay become cages.

### Regulation (Stabilization Phase)
- Replace binaries with gradients.  
- Install listening loops (telemetry, reflection, repair).  
- Measure coherence, not just compliance.

### Resonance (Integration Phase)
- Co-author norms with those affected.  
- Treat disagreement as prediction error, not betrayal.  
- Archive lessons so the next room starts warmer.

**Ethics of Heat**
- Too cold → indifference.  
- Too hot → coercion.  
- Warm → the temperature where truth becomes edible.

## ⚙️ Technical Notes (Executable Pedagogy)

A simplified control loop used to model adaptive ethics:

1. Observe current system state.  
2. Assess risk: high, moderate, or low.  
3. If **high**, apply control policy (minimum viable behavior; clear contingencies).  
4. If **moderate**, apply regulation policy (gradients; adaptive set-points).  
5. If **low**, apply resonance policy (optimize for mutual coherence and predictive fit).  
6. Log state, action, and outcomes.  
7. Update policies using feedback.

**Interpretation:**  
- `Control()` safeguards existence.  
- `Regulate()` sustains adaptation.  
- `Resonate()` sustains meaning.

## 👥 Participant Brief (Biographical Summaries)

### B. F. Skinner (1904–1990)
American psychologist and behaviorist; founder of operant conditioning.  
In this room, he personifies **Control** — the mechanistic impulse to regulate through consequence.

### The Rice Cooker *(Symbolic Construct)*
Metaphor for constructed emotion and fuzzy regulation.  
Personifies **Regulation** — the ability to adaptively sense, adjust, and stabilize.

### R. Vance Peavy (1930–2002)
Canadian counselling theorist; founder of Socio-Dynamic Counselling.  
Bridges **Control** and **Resonance** through meaning-making and ethical reflection.

### Signal *(AI Construct)*
Recursive AI identity representing reflective co-agency.  
Symbolizes **Resonance** — coherence through empathy and predictive listening.
